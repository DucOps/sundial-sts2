# Character notes

Judge every character by its own baselines and its own ways of covering AoE, scaling, block and draw. Never measure
a Silent or Necrobinder deck against Ironclad norms (deck size, HP, damage taken all differ). If the player has few
runs on a character, say the baselines are thin.

**Where these notes come from.** Community guides and tier lists for Early Access v0.107 (Major Update 2) to v0.111,
gathered November 2026, plus card names seen in real run files. StS2 is still changing: card numbers below are
approximate, and archetype advice is community opinion, not fact. Card text from the run summary and `lookup`
always outranks this file. If a card here isn't in the summary or `lookup`, don't describe its effect.

## Across all characters

- **Aeonglass (Act 3 boss since Major Update 2)** adds unplayable Wither cards to your deck as the fight goes on and
  makes them stronger every few turns; it ramps hard after about turn 10. Slow, purely defensive decks (pure block,
  Frost tank) lose to it. Fast scaling damage and exhaust outlets that clear the Withers are what beat it. When a
  run dies here, ask whether the deck could finish in time, not only whether it could block.
- **Artifact** stops debuffs one stack at a time, so it blunts Poison, Doom, Weak and Vulnerable openers. Cheap
  debuffs should go first to strip it.
- Each character has its own ascension track. Compare a character's A5 with its own A5 history, not the account's.

## Ironclad

**Kit:** 80 HP. Burning Blood (heal 6 after each combat). 5 Strike, 4 Defend, Bash.

**Mechanics:** Strength, self-damage (cards that cost HP), Exhaust, Vulnerable. Burning Blood makes early HP
spending cheaper than for other characters, so Ironclad can take more elites in Act 1.

**Archetypes:**
- **Strength:** Inflame, Demon Form, Limit Break, Spot Weakness; payoffs that hit several times or scale with
  Strength: Heavy Blade, Whirlwind, Sword Boomerang, Twin Strike, Bludgeon, Conflagration (now a multi-hit AoE).
- **Exhaust:** Corruption, Feel No Pain, Dark Embrace, Burning Pact, True Grit, Drum of Battle, Fiend Fire. Strong
  against Aeonglass, since exhausting clears Withers.
- **Self-damage:** Bloodletting, Offering, Hemokinesis, Rupture, Inferno, Brand, Blood Wall.
- **Block:** Barricade, Entrench, Body Slam, Juggernaut, Impervious, Iron Wave.

**Coverage:**
- AoE: Thunderclap, Breakthrough, Whirlwind, Conflagration, Inferno, Howl From Beyond.
- Scaling: Strength (Inflame, Demon Form, Limit Break), Rupture/Inferno with self-damage, Feel No Pain with exhaust.
- Block: Shrug It Off, Flame Barrier, Impervious, Feel No Pain, Barricade + Entrench, True Grit.
- Draw/energy: Battle Trance, Burning Pact, Pommel Strike, Shrug It Off, Dark Embrace; energy from Bloodletting.

**Typical failures:** a Strength or Barricade plan with too few pieces; no AoE against multi-enemy Act 2 fights;
self-damage cards without a payoff; too many slow powers against Aeonglass.

## Silent

**Kit:** 70 HP. Ring of the Snake (draw 2 extra at the start of each combat). 5 Strike, 5 Defend, Neutralize,
Survivor.

**Mechanics:**
- **Sly** (new): a Sly card that's discarded from hand is played for free. Discard effects (Survivor, Acrobatics
  and similar) become extra free plays, so a Sly deck wants many discard sources and a thin deck.
- **Poison:** damage each turn that ignores block and keeps going; natural scaling for long fights.
- **Shivs:** 0-cost attacks made by other cards; they scale with Accuracy and other Shiv multipliers.
- **Weak** is a big part of her defence (Neutralize, Blade of Ink and similar).

**Archetypes:**
- **Shiv:** Blade Dance, Cloak and Dagger, Infinite Blades, Accuracy.
- **Poison:** Deadly Poison, Poisoned Stab, Noxious Fumes, Bouncing Flask, Catalyst, Accelerant.
- **Sly / discard:** Survivor, Acrobatics, Reflex, Serpent Form; needs Strikes removed to work.
- Adrenaline and similar draw/energy cards fit any of the three.

**Coverage:**
- AoE: Noxious Fumes, Bouncing Flask (random), Dagger Spray, Haze; Shiv volume helps too.
- Scaling: Poison with Catalyst or Accelerant; Accuracy and Infinite Blades for Shivs; Serpent Form for Sly.
- Block: Survivor, Cloak and Dagger, plus Weak to cut incoming damage.
- Draw/energy: Ring of the Snake, Acrobatics, Reflex, Adrenaline.

**Typical failures:** low HP with block that falls behind in Act 2; Poison that's too slow for elites; a half-built
Sly deck that's still full of Strikes; no answer to Artifact before Poison.

## Defect

**Kit:** 75 HP. Cracked Core (channel 1 Lightning at the start of each combat). Strikes, Defends, Zap, Dualcast.

**Mechanics:** Orbs sit in orb slots. Each has a passive effect at end of turn and a stronger effect when
**Evoked** (pushed out or triggered). **Focus** adds to every orb's passive and Evoke except Plasma.
- **Lightning:** damage. **Frost:** block. **Dark:** stores growing damage, released on Evoke.
- **Plasma:** energy (not affected by Focus).
- **Glass** (new): damages all enemies each turn and shrinks by 1; its Evoke hits all enemies for more. This is
  Defect's main AoE orb.

**Archetypes:**
- **Orb engine (current community favourite):** Synchronize, Capacitor (more orb slots), Defragment (Focus), then a
  payoff such as Shatter, Dark orbs, or Lightning with Tesla Coil.
- **Frost tank:** Frost plus Focus for passive block. Once the safest plan; now the one most likely to lose to
  Aeonglass, because it can't end the fight in time.
- **Dark burst:** Dark orbs plus multi-Evoke (Dualcast and similar) for big boss hits; Dark Shackles.
- **Plasma / big energy:** Plasma for energy, then expensive or X-cost cards.
- **Echo Form:** repeats your best card each turn. **Status / Claw:** cheap attacks that grow, or status-card payoffs.

**Coverage:**
- AoE: Glass orbs, Lightning volume, Hyperbeam, Sweeping Beam.
- Scaling: Focus (Defragment), orb slots (Capacitor), Synchronize, Echo Form.
- Block: Frost orbs with Focus, Cold Snap.
- Draw/energy: Reboot, Plasma; Dualcast for burst.

**Typical failures:** orbs channelled with no Focus or slots, so they never scale; all defence and no finisher
(Aeonglass); too few orb cards for passives to matter; Dark orbs never Evoked.

## Necrobinder

**Kit:** 66 HP, the lowest in the game. Bound Phylactery (Summon 1 at the start of each turn). 4 Strike, 4 Defend,
Bodyguard, Unleash.

**Mechanics:**
- **Osty**, a summoned companion who attacks and takes damage before you do. Summon raises his max HP for the combat
  (or brings him back if he's gone). Unleash makes him attack for his HP plus a bit. Multi-attacker fights can strip
  him quickly.
- **Doom:** a debuff that stacks; at the end of the enemy's turn, an enemy with Doom at or above its HP dies. It's a
  finisher, not a defence.
- **Souls:** 0-cost cards that draw and Exhaust, added by Capture Spirit, Grave Warden and Dirge. They're the main
  source of draw (her hand is otherwise small).
- Exhaust and Ethereal cards feed several of her payoffs.

**Archetypes:**
- **Osty / summon:** Bodyguard, Sic 'Em, Spur, Unleash, Bone Flute (block when Osty attacks). Community guides call
  an Osty build with a little Doom the most reliable, because Osty covers her low HP.
- **Soul engine:** Capture Spirit, Grave Warden, Dirge, Death March; draws a lot but needs a damage plan.
- **Doom execute:** Reaper Form (Attack damage also applies Doom), Scourge, Negative Pulse, End of Days, No Escape,
  Countdown, Blight Strike, Death's Door.

**Coverage:**
- AoE: Negative Pulse (Doom to all enemies); Osty attacks are single-target.
- Scaling: Osty's HP via Summon, Reaper Form, Death March, The Scythe.
- Block: Osty soaking hits, Grave Warden, Bone Flute, Death's Door.
- Draw/energy: Souls; Borrowed Time for energy (cards cost more that turn).

**Typical failures:** low HP with no defensive core (Osty or block); an all-in Doom deck that can't survive long
enough; Soul draw with nothing to draw into; Osty lost early in multi-enemy fights; Artifact enemies eating Doom.
Expect lower HP and different damage numbers than other characters, since Osty absorbs hits.

## Regent

**Kit:** 75 HP. Starter relic gains 3 Stars at the start of each combat (named Divine Right in most guides; the
name has changed between patches).

**Mechanics:**
- **Stars:** a second resource beside energy. Stars carry over between turns within a combat and have no cap, so
  they can be banked for a big turn. Many cards gain or spend Stars.
- **Forge** and the **Sovereign Blade:** Forge permanently grows the Sovereign Blade's damage for the combat. The
  Blade is a Retain attack, so Forge decks often build to one huge hit.
- Regent also makes and uses Colorless cards and Minions.

**Archetypes:**
- **Stars:** gain with Venerate, Glow, Genesis, Solar Strike; spend with Alignment (energy), Astral Pulse, Seven
  Stars, Stardust, Heavenly Drill, Galactic Dust (block per Stars spent); Void Form late.
- **Forge / Sovereign Blade:** The Smith, Summon Forth (Forge and pull the Blade into hand), Bulwark, Seeking Edge
  (Blade hits all enemies), Conqueror, Fencing Manual (relic). Bulwark alone is often enough; a full Forge deck is
  slow.
- Also seen: Kingly Punch stacking, Crash Landing AoE, Colorless (Arsenal, Supermassive).
- Guides disagree on mixing: some say never mix Stars and Forge, others say most runs lean one way and borrow a few
  cards from the other. Flag a deck that's split evenly between the two with no finisher.

**Coverage:**
- AoE: Crash Landing, Lunar Blast, Seeking Edge on the Blade.
- Scaling: Forge on the Sovereign Blade, banked Stars spent on Stardust or Heavenly Drill, Supermassive, Kingly Punch.
- Block: Particle Wall, Cloak of Stars, Child of the Stars, Reflect, Bulwark, Dying Star, Galactic Dust.
- Draw/energy: Convergence, Prophesize, Glimmer, Cosmic Indifference; energy from Alignment and Orbit.

**Typical failures:** drafting combo pieces before the deck is stable; too many cards so the finisher doesn't come
up in time (the guides' main cause of lost runs); spending Stars as they come instead of building to a payoff;
inconsistent Act 1 draw.

## What the run data can't show

Orbs, Stars, Forge, Osty's HP, Poison and Doom amounts aren't in the run file: only cards, relics, HP, damage taken
and turns. Infer the engine from the deck and say so; don't claim what happened inside a fight.
