# Character notes

Judge every character by its own baselines and its own ways of covering AoE, scaling, block and draw. Never measure
a Silent or Necrobinder deck against Ironclad norms. If you know little about a character's cards, say so and rely
on card text from the summary and `lookup`. If the player has few runs on a character, say the baselines are thin.

| Character | Typical ways to cover AoE, scaling, block and draw |
|---|---|
| **Ironclad** | AoE: Thunderclap, Breakthrough, Inferno, Conflagration. Scaling: Strength (Inflame, Demon Form), self-damage payoffs (Rupture, Inferno). Block: Shrug It Off, Flame Barrier, Impervious, Feel No Pain/Barricade. Draw: Battle Trance, Burning Pact, Pommel Strike. |
| **Silent** | Built around Sly, Poison or Shivs. Poison is natural scaling; Shivs scale with multipliers; AoE from cards like Dagger Spray. |
| **Necrobinder** | Scaling via Doom and summons. |
| **Defect** | Orbs (Channel, Focus) for scaling and AoE. |
| **Regent** | Stars, Forge and Sovereign Blade. |
