# Necrobinder

**Coverage status: draft — not yet played.** Descriptive only, from game text and community sources. No archetype
definitions or card annotations; the run summary gives role counts from card text only.

## Kit and core resource

66 HP, the lowest of the five. Bound Phylactery: Summon 1 at the start of each turn. Starts with 4 Strike, 4 Defend,
Bodyguard (Summon 5), Unleash (Osty deals 6 damage plus his current HP).

Core resource: **Osty**, a summoned companion. Summon raises his HP; he attacks through cards such as Unleash and
takes hits for her. Because Osty absorbs damage, her damage-taken numbers aren't comparable with other characters.

## Mechanics

- **Doom:** at the end of the enemy's turn, an enemy with at least as much Doom as HP dies.
- **Souls:** token cards (draw 2, Exhaust) that other cards add.
- Exhaust and Ethereal cards feed several of her cards.

## What counts as real scaling

Summon that grows Osty over the fight, and Doom applied every turn. One-off Doom or Summon is front-loaded, not scaling.
