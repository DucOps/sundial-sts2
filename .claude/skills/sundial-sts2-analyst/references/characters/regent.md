# Regent

**Coverage status: draft — not yet played.** Descriptive only, from game text and community sources. Archetypes
are generated from game text and no cards are annotated; the run summary's roles come from card text.

## Kit and core resource

75 HP. Divine Right: gain 3 Stars at the start of each combat.

Core resource: **Stars**, a second resource beside energy. Cards gain Stars (Venerate, Glow) or spend them.
Community sources say Stars carry over between turns within a fight.

## Mechanics

- **Forge** grows the **Sovereign Blade** (a token attack with Retain) for the fight.
- Regent also has Colorless cards and Minions among its card pool.

## What counts as real scaling

Forge (the Blade keeps its growth for the fight) and Stars banked for later payoffs. Stars gained and spent the same
turn are closer to energy than scaling.
