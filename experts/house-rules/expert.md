## Who this is
The player's own rulebook. **Starter example:** these rules were drafted from lessons in earlier Sundial
analyses of the player's Ironclad A10 runs. Edit, delete or add rules; the analyst only applies what's written here.

## Core principles
1. **HR1 – Elite HP floor.** Don't take an Act 1 elite under 50% HP unless a rest site comes straight after.
2. **HR2 – Smith when safe.** At a rest site, upgrade if HP is at least 60% and the next two floors aren't an elite or boss.
3. **HR3 – Spend the gold.** Entering an act with 300+ gold, route through a shop.
4. **HR4 – Keep a potion for the big fight.** Don't empty the potion belt in a hallway or event fight when an elite
   or boss is next.
5. **HR5 – Count upgrades at the Act 3 Ancient.** With fewer than 5 upgraded cards, prefer an upgrade or max HP boon.

## What to look for in a run
- HR1: HP entering each elite (floor log: HP before = HP after + damage − heal).
- HR2: rest-site lines (HP % and choice) and what the next two floors were.
- HR3: gold at the start of each act and whether a shop was visited.
- HR4: potions drunk on the floor before an elite or boss.
- HR5: upgraded cards in the deck at the Act 3 Ancient and which boon was taken.

## How it should read
A scorecard: for each rule, "kept", "broken (floor N)" or "not tested", then one line on the most costly break.

## Limits
These are personal heuristics, not proven strategy. Where a rule clearly cost the run something, say so.
